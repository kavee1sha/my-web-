Guide to Japan's Most Common Foods

Sushi & Sashimi

Raw fish with rice or thinly sliced seafood.

Ramen

Japanese noodle soup with various broths and toppings.

Tempura

Deep-fried seafood and vegetables in a light batter.

Yakitori

Grilled skewered chicken with sauce or salt.

Tonkatsu

Deep-fried pork cutlet, crispy and tender.

Miso Soup

Traditional Japanese soup made with miso paste and dashi.

Udon

Thick wheat flour noodles served hot or cold.

Okonomiyaki

Japanese savory pan

Onigiri

Rice ball filled with various ingredients, wrapped in seaweed.

Soba Noodles

Japanese buckwheat noodles served hot or cold, often with a dipping sauce or in a broth.

Natto

Fermented soybeans, known for their strong flavor and sticky texture, often served with rice.
